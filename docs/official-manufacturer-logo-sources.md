## Migration rule

Only a logo obtained from the relevant manufacturer’s official public website or official media/brand resource may replace a manufacturer record’s `imageUrl`. The product page retains the manufacturer name as an accessible fallback whenever no verified logo exists or an asset fails to load.

## Current scope

The product-page logo component and data join are complete. The remaining manufacturer records require individual source verification; the existing imported logo URLs must not be represented as independently verified official sources until that review is completed.
