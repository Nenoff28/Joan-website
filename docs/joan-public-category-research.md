# Public Joan.bg Category Hierarchy Research

## Scope and method

This research records only the public navigation hierarchy needed to rebuild category navigation in the redesigned Joan storefront. It does not collect product records, product descriptions, pricing, customer data, or media from the original website.

## Source pages reviewed

| Public page | Findings captured |
|---|---|
| `https://joan.bg/` | Eleven top-level catalogue categories visible in the public menu |
| `https://joan.bg/строителни-материали/instrumenti` | Tools first-level subcategories visible in the public category filter |

## Research status

The public site confirms eleven top-level catalogue categories and their immediate children. The next research pass must inspect each immediate child category to capture second-level entries, such as the examples requested for Инструменти → Електроинструменти → Бормашини and Инструменти → Ръчни инструменти → Бъркалки.
