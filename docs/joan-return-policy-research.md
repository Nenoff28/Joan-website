# Public Joan Return-Process Research

Research completed on 2026-08-20 from the publicly accessible original Joan.bg pages. This record is used only to shape the redesigned customer-information page; it does not create a live return claim or submit customer data.

## Return-request workflow

The original public route at `https://joan.bg/index.php?route=account/return/add` is titled **„Връщане на продукти“** and asks customers to request an RMA incoming number. Its form groups the following information:

| Section | Publicly requested information |
|---|---|
| Order | First name, last name, email, phone, order number, and order date |
| Product | Product name, product code, and quantity |
| Return details | Reason for return, whether the product was opened, and an explanatory comment |
| Confirmation | Agreement with the terms before submitting the request |

The public reason options include a damaged-on-arrival product, another defect, incorrect product sent, ordering error, and another reason. The redesign should keep this as **guidance for contacting Joan**, not a transactional return form, because the current storefront checkout is explicitly non-transactional.

## Published withdrawal information

The original public terms page at `https://joan.bg/index.php?route=information/information/agree&information_id=5` states that a consumer may withdraw without giving a reason within **14 days** from receiving the goods, subject to the published legal conditions. It also states that returned goods should be presented in original packaging and preserved saleable condition with the relevant accessories, documents, and original receipt or invoice. Where withdrawal is exercised, the goods must be sent or handed back within **14 days** from notifying Joan of the decision; the consumer bears the risk of accidental loss or damage until handover.

The redesigned route should direct users to Joan’s support team for a return reference and refer to the full terms for applicable exceptions and detailed conditions. It must not imply that every product or circumstance is eligible.
