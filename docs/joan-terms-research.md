# Original Joan.bg terms research

**Source reviewed:** [Joan.bg — Условия за ползване](https://joan.bg/index.php?route=information/information/agree&information_id=5), accessed 2026-08-20. The project owner explicitly authorized use of this original-site material for the redesign.

## Public sections present on the original page

| Section | Source content to preserve in the redesigned presentation |
| --- | --- |
| General terms and definitions | Relationship between ЖОАН ЕООД, the site, account, online shop, users, buyers, consumers, personal data, cash-on-delivery, and acceptance of the terms. |
| Registration and general catalogue information | Browsing without registration, voluntary registration for selected functions, account information, product-information caveat, and price updates. |
| Orders, payment and delivery | Orders by users who accept the terms; cash-on-delivery wording; delivery in Bulgaria; published processing timelines and carrier caveat. |
| Inspection, complaints, withdrawal and warranty | Inspection on receipt, complaints under applicable consumer protection rules, legal conformity guarantee, and the published 14-day withdrawal details. |
| Personal data and cookies | Data categories, processing purposes, sharing with service partners or authorities where needed, security, retention, data-subject rights, complaint contact, and cookie controls. |
| Other and updates | Availability/price changes, misuse of the site, governing Bulgarian law and periodic updates. |

## Redesign decisions

The redesigned `/terms` page will retain the original public legal meaning and identifiers while making the text readable through a table of contents, short section summaries, and expandable detail blocks. It will explicitly state that the page reproduces the published terms for the Joan redesign and should be reviewed and updated by the business before live commercial checkout is enabled.

The existing public site is still a non-payment catalogue/request experience. Therefore, the terms page should not claim that this redesigned version currently accepts payment or automatically completes orders; it will link visitors to the relevant delivery and return-information pages and distinguish the current request-only flow from the original source wording.
