## ManoMano UX observations (for original interpretation only)

- Search is placed centrally in a compact, high-contrast header and is immediately reinforced by an all-products navigation trigger.
- Discovery combines timely hero/campaign content with focused product rails, top recommendations, category paths, and guide content.
- Product cards prioritize image, concise product name, credibility markers, visible pricing and promotional comparison, then minor metadata.
- Header actions are spatially separated from search to maintain a fast scan path: catalogue trigger → brand → search → professional/account/cart actions.

## Implications for Joan

- Build a search-first retail header with a compact category trigger and a clear mobile drawer.
- Use the real Joan taxonomy above as the category system and place promotional product information in structured cards.
- Present the supplier/store information as practical service information, not invented marketing claims.
- Reinterpret the marketplace principles using Joan Signal Red, neutral mineral surfaces, and original typography/layout.

## Kingfisher video treatment observations (for original interpretation only)

The homepage uses a full-bleed moving storefront scene as a restrained visual entry point, places only a small number of readable foreground actions over it, and lets the content section begin decisively after the video. For Joan, this supports a cinematic but quiet video hero with a strong dark overlay, one main Bulgarian message, and two purposeful routes into the catalogue rather than a copied composition.

## Joan company and delivery information

Joan states that it was founded in 2001 and began as a metal-trading company. Its current About page states that its own facilities comprise 7,200 square metres of covered area and 22,800 square metres of yard area, that it works with more than 300 firms in the city and region, and that it keeps more than 20,000 stock items. These facts can be presented as attributed company information, not extrapolated marketing claims. The delivery page describes dispatch after operator confirmation, shipment at the customer’s expense, same-day dispatch for weekday orders made before 16:00 and Saturday orders made before 12:00, plus exceptions for access, out-of-stock items, force majeure, and natural events.
